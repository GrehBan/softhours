About Soft Hours

Soft Hours is an independent game studio. We make small, calm games for the quiet hours of the day: the minutes before sleep, a pause on the way home, a slow morning.

Our games ask nothing of you. There are no scores to chase, no timers, no daily streaks to keep and no punishment for being away. You play when you want, as long as you want.

We don't show ads, don't track players and don't ask for accounts. What the game remembers stays on your phone.

Our first game is Purr, a black cat who lives on your phone and slowly learns to trust you.
