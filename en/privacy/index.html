Purr privacy policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Purr does not collect, send or share any personal data. Everything the game remembers stays on your phone.

What the game stores on your phone

  • The cat's progress: trust, age, character, habits and your settings.
  • Album postcards: small pictures the game draws of your moments with the cat.

This data lives in the app's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling Purr or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it.

Saving postcards

If you tap "Save to gallery", Purr saves that postcard as an image to your phone's gallery, in a Purr album. The game never writes there without your tap, and it doesn't read your other files or send anything anywhere. On Android 9 and older this needs storage permission, which the game asks for once.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Purr schedules short reminders about the cat on your phone itself: at most one a day, and none during the quiet hours you choose. No server is involved. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in Android settings at any time. To keep reminders after a restart, the game asks Android to restore them when the phone boots.

Vibration

Purr uses the vibration motor so you can feel the cat purr. This collects no data.

No network, no ads, no analytics

Purr does not request internet access. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party services that collect data. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Where you get the game

If you download Purr from Google Play or Telegram, that service handles the download under its own privacy policy. From Google Play we receive only the anonymous aggregate statistics that Google provides to every developer.

Children

Purr collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
