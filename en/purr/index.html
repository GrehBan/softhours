A calm cat game for Android

Purr

A black cat who lives on your phone.

Stroke him with your finger. He feels where you touch him, which way and how fast, and answers with a pose, a purr and a soft vibration. Day by day he trusts you a little more, grows up, picks up habits and keeps an album of your moments together.

What he does

  • Feels your hand. Head, back, chin and belly each mean something different, and he likes it along the fur.
  • Purrs you can feel. The purr comes with a gentle vibration, so the phone hums in your hand.
  • Trust grows quietly. There is no meter on screen. You notice it when he comes closer and lets you stroke his back.
  • An album of moments. First meeting, first purr, the first nap under your hand: each one becomes a postcard.
  • Palettes and coats. Seven palettes from Classic to Twilight and seven coats from black to ginger.
  • He never sulks. Come back after a day or a month. Nothing is lost and nobody is punished.
